## Duplicates

Duplicate files were not discarded from the recovery section. Multiple proposal, prototype-overview, V2-framework, PDF, notebook, spreadsheet, ZIP, and dataset copies existed in Downloads and the organized Documents archive.

The curated sections choose clearly named working copies. `90_ALL_FOUND_VERSIONS` preserves recovered source-location copies so earlier revisions can still be examined.

Use `SHA256_MANIFEST.txt` to prove whether two files are byte-for-byte identical. Files with the same SHA-256 value have identical contents even when their filenames differ.
