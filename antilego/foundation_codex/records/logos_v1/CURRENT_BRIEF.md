# LOGOS Brief

Status: Completed V1 asset; publication status unresolved
Owner: Caleb and Shadi Alazzeh
Last updated: 2026-07-19

LOGOS - Logical Optimization for Global Outcome Systems - detects probability-logic violations across related Polymarket contracts and projects observed prices onto a nearest coherent probability system using convex optimization.

Verified historical prototype figures from the handoff: 132 snapshots, approximately 11 hours, 58 contracts, five market families, and 7,656 observations.

Safe framing: undergraduate quantitative research combining probability, convex optimization, prediction-market structure, and empirical analysis.

Unsafe framing: proven arbitrage, alpha, profitability, or mathematically novel theory.

Stored artifacts include writeup, build plan, Python, notebook, and market-family workbooks.
