How to read a signal

A violation is a lead,
not a guaranteed trade.

Antilego compares related probabilities with mathematical rules. When the executable order-book prices conflict, it records the contracts and the size of the discrepancy. Thin or empty books stay on the watchlist as illiquid.

Signals use depth-weighted bids and asks for at least five shares. Always verify fees, resolution rules, and whether the discrepancy still exists before risking money.
